Explore how models predict profitability and learn from fictional cattle ranches.

Without JavaScript, the lesson is still readable: linear regression predicts annual dollar profit from cattle sold; logistic regression estimates the chance of positive profit from cattle sales and feed cost. Five-fold validation fits five fresh models inside 72 development ranches, each time predicting the excluded group. A final classifier fits all 72 and is assessed once on 18 untouched ranches.

Enable JavaScript to move the sliders, explore real gradient updates, and run validation locally.
