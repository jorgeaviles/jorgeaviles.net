Explore linear regression, logistic regression, and model evaluation with independently synthetic data. Models train locally in the browser using gradient descent. Validation rankings derive from the logistic model's predictions; threshold and review-budget policies do not retrain it. These small educational samples do not measure real-world operational performance.
