This example uses deterministic, independently fabricated records. It contains no CPCS records, no real model performance, and no trained model.

Choose how many records to review from the top of a fixed ranking. **Precision** is the share of reviewed records labeled positive. **Recall** is the share of all positive records captured by that selection. With no selection, precision has no denominator; this demonstration displays both metrics as zero.

A larger selection can find more positives while also adding negatives. The table makes that trade-off visible. The labels and ranking are invented for explanation; they cannot establish whether a real scoring system is useful or fair.
