The 2014 study by **Anand N. Asthana** investigates machine-learning approaches to predicting cattle-ranch profitability in Latin America. I contributed research assistance to the project and am acknowledged as **J. Avilés** on page 478.

Asthana, A. N. (2014). _Profitability Prediction in Cattle Ranches in Latin America: A Machine Learning Approach_. _Global Veterinaria_, 13(4), 473–495. DOI: 10.5829/idosi.gv.2014.13.04.1179.

The study uses a vector space model and kernel ridge regression, evaluated with randomized ten-fold cross-validation, to examine the relationship between ranch characteristics and profitability. These methods connect a business forecasting question with statistical learning. The publisher’s paper provides the full methodology and acknowledgment.
