## From quantitative research to analytical systems

My interests have developed from investigating economic and business questions to building the data preparation, models, and applications that support analytical work. Banking experience added a focus on data quality and business requirements; my current work connects those concerns with data engineering and software development.

The [technical case studies](/work/) describe that work in practice. The [audit-prioritization modeling case study](/work/machine-learning-audit-prioritization/) covers an experimental program of feature engineering, supervised learning, grouped validation, calibration, and backtesting. Explore a separate [interactive profitability example](/lab/) to predict annual profit, estimate profitability, and test predictions with five-fold validation using fictional ranches.
