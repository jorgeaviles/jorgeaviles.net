## Research background

At CENTRUM Católica Graduate Business School, I worked as a Graduate Research Assistant from April 2012 to September 2014. I supported faculty research involving profitability forecasting, consumer spending, conjoint analysis, and pricing. I prepared and validated datasets, performed statistical analysis in SPSS and Stata, and designed and analyzed discrete-choice and conjoint experiments. Logit modeling helped examine consumer trade-offs and willingness to pay.
