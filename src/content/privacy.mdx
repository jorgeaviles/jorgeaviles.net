## Privacy & measurement

When enabled on the public site, [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/)
uses a browser beacon to measure page visits and loading performance. Cloudflare
does not track individual visitors across its customers’ websites.

The Lab’s data and calculations stay in your browser; its slider settings and
model results are not submitted as analytics events. There are no advertising
scripts, visitor accounts, or third-party embeds. Contact links open email or an
external professional profile.

<h3 id="portfolio-assistant-privacy">Portfolio assistant</h3>

When activated, the portfolio assistant sends the question you choose to submit,
along with relevant passages from approved public website content, to a separate
Cloudflare Worker and Workers AI. Each question stands alone: this site does not
save conversation history, use browser storage for questions, or send questions
as Web Analytics events. Please do not submit personal, confidential, or agency
information.

The Worker uses the connecting IP address transiently for request throttling. It
does not send that address to the AI model or create a visitor profile. Application
prompt/response logging is disabled. Cloudflare processes requests and operational
metadata under its own policies; see [Workers AI data usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/).
This is separate from the browser-only Lab and ordinary static page visits.

The AI selects published passages rather than writing new factual claims. Answers
link to their sources and may be incomplete or irrelevant. If the sources do not
support an answer, or the free service is unavailable, use the public pages directly.

<h3 id="portfolio-mcp-privacy">Public MCP integration</h3>

Compatible AI clients can request curated public portfolio passages from the
separate read-only MCP endpoint. MCP retrieval does not invoke Workers AI. The
tools cannot modify content or access private accounts, files, or employer
databases. The application does not intentionally store conversation histories
or log request contents. Cloudflare processes network requests and operational
metadata; the connecting IP address is used transiently for throttling. Your
connected AI client follows its own data practices. Do not send confidential
information. See [connection instructions and scope](/mcp/).
